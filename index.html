Price halts or fails to displace proportionally. Passive limit orders absorb the aggressors.

Aggressive flow exhausts. Counter-delta emerges and price reclaims the value boundary.

Family 2: ACCEPTANCE (Trend Continuation)
1. High Velocity Break

Price thrusts across VAH or VAL into an LVN (thin liquidity pocket) with strong positive/negative delta.

2. Value Acceptance Outside

Candle closes and accepts outside the previous value area without immediate sharp rejection back inside.

3. Clean Retest with Flow Defense

Price pulls back to retest the broken boundary from the outside, supported by persistent directional delta.

EXECUTION: Enter on retest confirmation.
SL: Back inside the value boundary.
TP: Next higher/lower session HVN or liquidity pool.
